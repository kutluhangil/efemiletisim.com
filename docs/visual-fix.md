# Complete Product Catalog Visual & Specification Audit

I want you to perform a **complete product-by-product audit of the entire e-commerce website**.

Website:

https://efem2.vercel.app/

The purpose of this task is to make sure that **every product page accurately represents the real product being sold**, especially regarding:

- Product images
- Product variants
- Colors
- Sizes
- Models
- Storage/capacity options where applicable
- Product descriptions
- Highlighted features
- Technical specifications

This must NOT be treated as a simple image replacement task.

You must inspect the entire product catalog **one product at a time**, research each real product carefully, compare the website data with reliable real-world information, and correct inconsistencies.

---

# 1. Audit Every Product Individually

Go through **every product currently available on the website**.

Do not assume that existing product information is correct.

For each product, identify:

- Exact brand
- Exact product name
- Exact model
- Exact generation/version
- Exact size
- Available colors
- Available variants
- Storage/capacity options if applicable
- Connectivity version if applicable
- Other selectable options displayed on the website

Treat every product as a separate research task.

Do not apply generic information from another model in the same product family.

For example:

Apple Watch Series 11 46mm GPS

must be researched specifically as that exact model.

Do not accidentally use specifications or images belonging to:

- another Apple Watch generation
- another case size
- Cellular instead of GPS
- another material
- another product family
- another regional/model variant

The same principle applies to Samsung, Huawei, Xiaomi, JBL and every other brand.

---

# 2. Verify Product Variants Against Reality

This is one of the most important parts of the task.

Inspect all selectable options currently displayed on each product page.

Examples:

- Color
- Case color
- Band color
- Size
- Band size
- Storage
- Model
- Connectivity
- Capacity
- Other product-specific variants

Research whether those variants **actually exist for that exact product**.

Never preserve a variant merely because it already exists in the current website data.

If the website says:

- Jet Black
- Rose Gold
- Space Gray

verify that those are genuine options for that exact model.

If a variant does not actually exist, correct it.

If an important real variant is missing, add it where appropriate.

The final variant list must represent the actual product being sold.

---

# 3. Product Images Must Match the Exact Product

Audit every existing image.

An image is NOT acceptable simply because it looks similar to the product.

It must represent the correct:

**brand + product + generation + model + size + variant**

Do not use images of visually similar older or newer models.

Do not use generic smartwatch, headphone or accessory images.

Do not use concept renders or AI-generated approximations when authentic product imagery is available.

---

# 4. Images Must Correspond to the Available Variants

This is especially important.

If a product has multiple selectable colors or variants, the gallery should visually represent those options.

For example, if the website offers:

- Jet Black
- Rose Gold
- Space Gray

I expect appropriate images representing those actual variants whenever authentic images are available.

Do NOT create a situation where the customer selects "Rose Gold" while every image shows a black product.

The customer should be able to visually understand what each major variant looks like.

---

# 5. Do Not Use Only One Image

Each product should have a proper professional product gallery.

Whenever sufficient authentic images are available, find multiple useful images such as:

1. Primary/front product image
2. Alternative angle
3. Side or rear view
4. Important available color/variant
5. Additional product angle
6. Detail image where useful

The exact number does not have to be identical for every product.

Use judgment based on the product and available official/high-quality material.

However, avoid leaving products with only one image when several useful authentic images are available.

---

# 6. Image Quality Requirements

The entire catalog should have a visually consistent, premium e-commerce appearance.

Prefer images that are:

- High resolution
- Sharp
- Clean
- Professionally photographed/rendered
- Correctly proportioned
- Free from unnecessary text
- Free from watermarks
- Free from retailer branding
- Free from promotional badges
- Free from unrelated backgrounds
- Visually suitable for an e-commerce product gallery

Whenever possible, prefer:

1. Manufacturer/brand product imagery
2. Manufacturer press/media assets
3. Reliable authorized retailer imagery
4. Other trustworthy high-quality sources

Avoid low-quality marketplace images whenever better sources exist.

---

# 7. Maintain Visual Consistency Across the Store

The images should not look like they were randomly collected from different websites.

Try to maintain consistency in:

- Background
- Product scale
- Aspect ratio
- Framing
- Resolution
- Image quality
- Product positioning
- Visual style

Prefer clean manufacturer product renders with neutral or transparent/light backgrounds whenever possible.

The first/main image of every product is particularly important.

The product should:

- Be clearly visible
- Be centered appropriately
- Fill the available area naturally
- Not appear extremely small
- Not be unnecessarily cropped
- Not have excessive empty space

---

# 8. Avoid Duplicate and Redundant Images

Do not download multiple images that are effectively identical.

Each gallery image should provide some value.

For example:

- Different angle
- Different color
- Different configuration
- Different product detail

Do not create a gallery containing five nearly identical front-facing images.

---

# 9. Technical Specifications Must Be Factually Correct

Audit the entire **Technical Specifications** section for every product.

Do not trust the current specifications automatically.

Research the exact product and verify every important specification.

Depending on the product category, verify information such as:

### Smartwatches

- Display
- Display technology
- Display size
- Case size
- Processor
- Battery life
- Water resistance
- Dust resistance
- GPS/GNSS
- Bluetooth
- Wi-Fi
- Sensors
- Health features
- Compatibility
- Materials
- Weight
- Dimensions
- Charging
- Connectivity

### Headphones / Earbuds

- Driver size/type
- ANC
- Transparency/Ambient mode
- Bluetooth version
- Supported codecs
- Battery life
- Charging case battery
- Charging time
- Fast charging
- Microphones
- Water/dust resistance
- Weight
- Connectivity
- Multipoint support
- Controls

### Other Products

Determine the technically important specifications for that specific category and verify them individually.

---

# 10. Never Invent Technical Specifications

If reliable information cannot be confirmed, do NOT guess.

Do not generate plausible-looking specifications.

For example, never assume:

"Bluetooth 5.3"

simply because similar products use Bluetooth 5.3.

Likewise, do not assume:

- battery life
- processor
- screen technology
- IP rating
- water resistance
- dimensions
- weight
- health sensors
- supported codecs
- charging speed

Every specification must belong to the **exact product/model**.

When sources disagree, prioritize authoritative manufacturer documentation.

---

# 11. Correct the Product Description

Audit the description shown on every product page.

The description should accurately explain the exact product.

It should be:

- Short
- Useful
- Natural
- Factually accurate
- Product-specific
- Suitable for an e-commerce website

Remove incorrect claims.

Do not use generic marketing text that could describe ten different products.

Important product characteristics may be mentioned, but they must be verifiable.

---

# 12. Verify Highlighted Features

The website displays important features separately from the full technical specification table.

Audit these as well.

For example:

- Display
- Processor
- Battery
- Durability

These should contain the most useful customer-facing specifications for that exact product.

They must agree with the detailed Technical Specifications section.

Never allow contradictions such as:

Highlighted feature:

"Battery: up to 24 hours"

Technical Specifications:

"Battery: up to 18 hours"

There must be a **single consistent source of truth** for the product.

---

# 13. Cross-Check Images Against Text

After researching each product, perform a consistency check between:

**Product title  
↕  
Variants  
↕  
Images  
↕  
Description  
↕  
Highlighted features  
↕  
Technical specifications**

All six must describe the same real-world product.

For example, if the title specifies a 46mm model, make sure:

- Images represent the appropriate model
- Description refers to the correct size
- Technical specifications correspond to that size
- Available variants actually exist for that model

---

# 14. Special Attention to Visually Similar Products

Be extremely careful with products where generations look similar.

Examples include:

- Apple Watch generations
- Samsung Galaxy Watch generations
- Huawei Watch/Fit generations
- AirPods generations
- Galaxy Buds generations
- Xiaomi wearables
- JBL headphone generations

Do not determine the product solely from visual similarity.

Verify the exact model before using an image.

---

# 15. Research Before Downloading

For every product, use this workflow:

**STEP 1 — Identify**

Determine the exact product and existing website variants.

**STEP 2 — Research**

Research the real product using trustworthy sources.

**STEP 3 — Verify**

Determine the genuine:

- Colors
- Variants
- Specifications
- Features
- Product appearance

**STEP 4 — Compare**

Compare the verified information against the current website.

**STEP 5 — Find Images**

Find high-quality authentic images corresponding to the correct model and variants.

**STEP 6 — Correct**

Correct incorrect or incomplete product information.

**STEP 7 — Validate**

Review the finished product page and ensure that everything represents the same real product.

Only then move to the next product.

---

# 16. Do Not Blindly Trust Search Result Titles

A Google/Bing/image-search result containing the product name does NOT automatically mean the image is correct.

Open and inspect the source when necessary.

Watch for incorrect listings where sellers use names such as:

"Series 11 compatible"

or images from another generation.

Search results, marketplaces and reseller titles can be inaccurate.

Cross-reference questionable information.

---

# 17. Source Priority

When researching product facts, prioritize sources approximately in this order:

1. Official manufacturer website
2. Official manufacturer technical specifications
3. Official support documentation
4. Official product manuals
5. Manufacturer press/media resources
6. Major authorized retailers
7. Highly reputable technology publications/databases

Avoid using random blogs, SEO pages, marketplace sellers or user-generated listings as the primary source for technical specifications when authoritative sources are available.

---

# 18. Example of the Problem I Want Fixed

A current product page may offer color selections such as:

- Jet Black
- Rose Gold
- Space Gray

while the gallery images do not properly represent those options.

This is exactly the type of inconsistency you must find throughout the entire catalog.

Do not only fix this example.

Use it as a pattern for detecting similar problems across **every product on the website**.

Another example would be a smartwatch whose product page says it has certain health sensors, battery life or display specifications that actually belong to another generation.

That must also be detected and corrected.

---

# 19. Quality Over Quantity

Do not add images merely to increase the image count.

I would rather have:

**4 excellent, accurate images**

than:

**8 mediocre or questionable images.**

However, when several genuine colors/variants exist, try to visually cover those variants.

---

# 20. Final Product-Level Validation

Before considering ANY product complete, confirm:

- Exact product model is identified
- Product title represents the correct model
- Existing colors are genuine
- Existing variants are genuine
- Missing important variants have been considered
- Main image represents the exact product
- Gallery contains multiple useful images where available
- Images represent important available variants
- No image belongs to another model/generation
- No low-resolution images remain when better alternatives exist
- No watermarked images are used
- No unnecessary duplicate images exist
- Description is factually accurate
- Highlighted specifications are accurate
- Technical specification table is accurate
- No technical information was guessed
- Images and product information agree with each other

If ANY of these checks fail, the product is not finished.

---

# 21. Full Catalog Validation

After completing all products, perform one additional pass across the entire store.

Look specifically for:

- Wrong product images
- Wrong generations
- Duplicate images
- Low-resolution images
- Missing color images
- Color/image mismatches
- Variant inconsistencies
- Incorrect specifications
- Generic descriptions
- Contradictory information
- Inconsistent gallery quality
- Products with suspiciously incomplete galleries

Correct anything discovered during this second pass.

---

# 22. Important Rules

Do NOT:

- Guess specifications
- Invent product variants
- Invent colors
- Use images from visually similar models
- Use AI-generated substitutes for real product photography when authentic imagery exists
- Use low-resolution images when high-resolution alternatives exist
- Use watermarked retailer images
- Assume existing website information is correct
- Stop after fixing only obvious products
- Change unrelated parts of the website

The goal is not simply to make the website "look better."

The goal is:

> **Every product page must accurately and professionally represent the exact real-world product that the customer is purchasing.**

Treat accuracy as the highest priority, followed by image quality and visual consistency.

Work systematically through the **entire catalog, product by product**, until every product has been researched, corrected, and validated.
